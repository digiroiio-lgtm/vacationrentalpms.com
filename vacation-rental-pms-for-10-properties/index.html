Frequently asked questions

What PMS is best for 10 vacation rental properties?

For 10 vacation rental properties, the best PMS is one that becomes the operational core: multi-property calendar, housekeeping and turnover management, role-based team workflows, portfolio and property-level reporting, and daily visibility into exceptions like unassigned jobs. Prioritize operational fit over feature count.

How does a PMS change operations at 10 vacation rental properties?

At 10 properties, operational exceptions happen more often and manual coordination is harder to audit. A PMS should connect reservations, calendars, turnovers and team tasks automatically, becoming the reliable operating source of truth rather than just a booking record.

What should a vacation rental PMS include at 10 properties?

Essential features at 10 properties: multi-property calendar and reservation management, automated turnover creation, cleaner assignment and status tracking, role-based team access, portfolio KPI reporting (ADR, occupancy, RevPAR), and integration with pricing and channel management tools.

How do I choose between vacation rental PMS options at 10 properties?

Compare on: daily operational usability, how quickly your team can spot problems, depth of housekeeping and team workflows, reporting visibility, total cost at your current and projected property count, and how the platform scales toward 20 or 50 properties.
