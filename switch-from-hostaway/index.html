SWITCHING PMS

How to Switch from Hostaway

Map the current operating system before moving it. A successful migration preserves booking integrity while rebuilding the workflows your team relies on.

Before cutover

Export and validate property data

Protect future reservation data

Document channel connections

Map cleaning and team workflows

List required integrations and reports

Test the new workflow

Rehearse common scenarios such as a new reservation, date change, cancellation, turnover and reporting cycle before moving the full portfolio.

Use switching criteria

The new PMS should solve the reasons that triggered the migration. Confirm those requirements directly rather than assuming every alternative is a better fit.

Frequently asked questions

How do you switch from Hostaway?

Switching from Hostaway requires exporting property and reservation data, documenting all channel connections and team workflows, then rebuilding them in the destination PMS before running a controlled cutover.

What data needs to migrate when leaving Hostaway?

At minimum: future reservations, property details, guest records and channel connection credentials. Cleaner workflows, automation rules and reporting configurations also need rebuilding in the new system.
