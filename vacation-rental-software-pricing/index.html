VACATION RENTAL SOFTWARE PRICING

What should vacation rental software cost?

PMS pricing can be flat, per property, portfolio-based or transaction-based. Compare the total operating cost, not just the number displayed on the pricing page.

Short answer: Vacation rental software pricing ranges from roughly $20–$200+/month depending on portfolio size and model. The main structures are flat monthly subscriptions, per-property fees, portfolio tier pricing, and transaction-based fees — compare total annual cost including onboarding and integrations, not just the headline rate.

Common vacation rental software pricing models

Flat monthly subscription

A fixed subscription is easy to budget but may include property limits, feature tiers or paid add-ons.

Per-property pricing

The monthly bill grows directly with the number of active properties. This can be efficient for small portfolios but should be modeled carefully before scaling.

Portfolio-based pricing

Properties are grouped into bands. The effective cost per property can decline as the portfolio grows. Avrenor Stays plans, for example, use portfolio tiers ranging from $99/month for 1–5 properties to $799/month for 76–100 properties.

Transaction and booking fees

Some platforms combine subscription fees with booking, payment or direct-booking charges. These can materially change total cost at higher booking volumes.

How to calculate real PMS cost

Model the software at your current property count and again at your expected portfolio size 12 to 24 months from now. Include subscription, transaction costs and required add-ons. Then divide the annual total by properties and completed reservations to understand the real operating cost.

Example of portfolio-based pricing

Avrenor Stays currently follows a proposed portfolio ladder of $99 for 1–5 properties, $149 for 6–10, $249 for 11–20, $399 for 21–40, $599 for 41–75 and $799 for 76–100, with volume pricing above 100 properties.
