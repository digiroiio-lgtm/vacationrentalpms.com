Do not assume compatibility

Smart-lock support varies by PMS, lock provider and integration. Confirm current compatibility directly before buying hardware or changing software.

Design for exceptions

Automation is useful only if staff can quickly identify failed codes, changed stays or properties requiring manual intervention.
