Frequently asked questions

What PMS is best for 100 vacation rental properties?

For 100 vacation rental properties, choose a PMS that handles enterprise-scale operations reliably: portfolio-wide calendar, granular team permissions, scalable housekeeping workflows, property-level financial analytics, deep integrations, and a proven migration and implementation process.

What is different about managing 100 properties in a PMS?

At 100 properties, workflow quality and data integrity matter more than individual features. The system must handle daily arrivals, same-day turnovers, team exceptions, reporting deadlines and portfolio growth without degrading. Evaluate this under realistic operating conditions, not a sales demo.

How do I plan a PMS migration for 100 vacation rental properties?

A 100-property migration requires: detailed data export from the current system, property and reservation mapping, team account and permissions setup, channel reconnection with minimal blackout, integration validation, and parallel running if feasible. Budget 4–8 weeks of implementation time.

What integration requirements come with a 100-property portfolio?

At 100 properties, plan for: OTA channel API integrations, direct booking engine, accounting system (QuickBooks, Xero), dynamic pricing tool, smart lock or access control, and potentially a custom API for internal business systems or owner portals. Confirm all current integrations with each vendor.
