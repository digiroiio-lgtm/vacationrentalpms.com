SWITCHING PMS

How to Switch from Guesty

Treat a PMS switch as an operational migration, not a login change. Inventory the data, workflows, integrations and team processes that must survive the move.

Migration checklist

Properties and unit data

Future reservations and guest records

Channel connections and availability

Turnover and team workflows

Financial and performance history

Integrations, exports and permissions

Run a controlled cutover

Define ownership, validation steps and a rollback plan. Confirm how future bookings, rates and availability will move before disconnecting the existing system.

Evaluate the destination first

Use your reasons for leaving as acceptance criteria for the new PMS. Verify them in a demo or test environment before migration.

Frequently asked questions

How do you switch from Guesty?

Switching from Guesty requires inventorying your properties, future reservations and channel connections, defining requirements for the new PMS, migrating data, and running a controlled cutover that confirms booking integrity before disconnecting Guesty.

How long does it take to migrate from Guesty?

Migration from Guesty typically takes 2–8 weeks depending on portfolio size, channel connections and data volume. A low-season cutover window reduces operational risk.

What are the biggest risks when switching from Guesty?

The primary risks are losing future reservation data, creating calendar availability conflicts during cutover, and disrupting team workflows. A methodical migration checklist and parallel validation period significantly reduce these risks.
