How to Switch from Guesty

Treat a PMS switch as an operational migration, not a login change. Inventory the data, workflows, integrations and team processes that must survive the move.

Migration checklist

Properties and unit data

Future reservations and guest records

Channel connections and availability

Turnover and team workflows

Financial and performance history

Integrations, exports and permissions

Run a controlled cutover

Define ownership, validation steps and a rollback plan. Confirm how future bookings, rates and availability will move before disconnecting the existing system.

Evaluate the destination first

Use your reasons for leaving as acceptance criteria for the new PMS. Verify them in a demo or test environment before migration.
