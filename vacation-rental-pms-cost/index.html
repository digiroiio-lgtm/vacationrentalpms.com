VACATION RENTAL PMS COST

How much does a vacation rental PMS cost?

The real cost depends on portfolio size, pricing model, booking volume and which operational modules are included in the base subscription.

Short answer: A vacation rental PMS typically costs $99–$800+/month depending on portfolio size and pricing model. Always calculate total annual cost — subscription, onboarding, transaction fees, integrations and optional modules — rather than comparing headline monthly rates.

PMS cost is more than the monthly subscription

Vacation rental platforms can charge a fixed monthly amount, a fee per property, portfolio tiers, booking fees or a combination of these models. A low advertised subscription can therefore become more expensive once required modules and transaction costs are included.

Cost by portfolio size

For small portfolios, simplicity and a predictable minimum bill usually matter most. As portfolios grow, the effective cost per property becomes a more useful comparison metric. At larger scale, implementation, integrations, API access and accounting requirements can outweigh small differences in headline subscription price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a vacation rental PMS cost?

Vacation rental PMS costs typically range from $99 to $800+ per month depending on portfolio size and pricing model. Portfolio-tiered platforms like Avrenor Stays start at $99/month for 1–5 properties and scale to custom pricing for 100+. Always compare total cost including onboarding, transaction fees and integrations.

What factors affect the total cost of a vacation rental PMS?

The total cost depends on: base subscription model (flat, per-property, or portfolio tier), number of properties, booking volume if transaction fees apply, required integrations and API access, onboarding and implementation, payment processing, and optional modules like accounting or owner portals.

Is per-property or portfolio pricing cheaper for vacation rentals?

Portfolio tier pricing becomes more cost-effective at scale. With portfolio tiers, the effective cost per property declines as you move up — for example, $99/month for 5 properties is $20/property, while $249/month for 20 properties is $12/property. Per-property pricing grows linearly and can exceed portfolio tiers at 10+ properties.

What PMS costs do operators most often overlook?

Common overlooked costs include: implementation and data migration, payment processing fees on direct bookings, API access for third-party integrations, per-user or team seat fees, channel connectivity costs, and ongoing support tiers. Always request a full-cost quote, not just the subscription price.
