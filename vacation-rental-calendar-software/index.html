MULTI-PROPERTY CALENDAR

Vacation rental calendar software for the entire portfolio.

See bookings, availability, direct stays and out-of-order inventory across multiple properties without jumping between channel calendars.

Short answer: Vacation rental calendar software shows all properties and booking channels on a single timeline — so operators see occupancy patterns, availability gaps and channel mix across the entire portfolio without toggling between individual listing calendars.

Why a unified vacation rental calendar matters

Operators managing multiple units need more than a single-listing calendar. A portfolio calendar should make occupancy patterns, booking gaps, unavailable inventory and channel mix visible across every unit in one horizontal timeline.

Core calendar capabilities

Multi-property and multi-unit view

Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and direct booking visibility

Arrival and departure dates

Out-of-order and out-of-service inventory

Unit search and filtering

Bedroom, bathroom and sleeps filters

Fast reservation creation

Calendar as an operations surface

The calendar should not exist in isolation. Moving from a booking to the associated guest, property, turnover and reporting workflow reduces operational fragmentation and makes the PMS more useful as the system of record.

Avrenor multi-calendar

Avrenor displays portfolio units on a single timeline with channel-coded reservation bars and operational inventory states. Managers can search units, filter the portfolio and review overlapping demand across dozens of units from one screen.

Who benefits most?

A unified calendar is particularly valuable for property managers, aparthotel operators and multi-property hosts where availability must be understood at portfolio level rather than one listing at a time.
