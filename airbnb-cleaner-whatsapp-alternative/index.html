An Airbnb Cleaner WhatsApp Alternative Built Around Turnovers

WhatsApp is excellent for conversation. It is a weak system of record when cleaner assignments, dates, photos, status and payments are buried in chat history.

Why cleaner chats become operational debt

As a portfolio grows, managers need to know which jobs are assigned, which remain unassigned, when the cleaning window starts, whether work is complete and what the cleaner should be paid.

What structured turnover software adds

Cleaning jobs linked to reservation dates

Cleaner assignment and unassigned-job visibility

Checkout-to-check-in windows

Job status and completion tracking

Photo or proof-of-work workflow

Cleaner pay and outstanding balances

Keep chat for exceptions, not the database

Teams can still communicate by chat. The operational record should live somewhere structured so the manager does not have to search a conversation to answer basic questions about today's turnovers.
