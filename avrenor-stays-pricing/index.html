AVRENOR STAYS PRICING 2026

How much does Avrenor Stays cost?

Avrenor uses portfolio-based monthly pricing, starting at $49/month for operators managing up to three properties and moving to custom volume pricing above 100 properties.

Short answer: Avrenor Stays uses portfolio-based monthly pricing starting at $49/month for 1–3 properties, rising to $799/month for 76–100 properties, with custom volume pricing above 100.

Avrenor pricing explained

The pricing model is structured around portfolio size rather than charging the same per-property rate at every level. As the portfolio grows, the effective monthly software cost per property declines.

Which Avrenor plan fits your portfolio?

1–10 properties: suited to individual hosts and small operators consolidating core workflows ($49/mo for 1–3, $129/mo for 4–10). 11–40: aimed at professional managers coordinating a growing operating team. 41–100: creates lower effective per-property economics for larger portfolios. Operators above 100 properties move to volume pricing.

What to confirm before buying

Software price is only one part of a PMS decision. Confirm onboarding, integrations, payment processing, direct-booking requirements, support and any optional modules directly with the vendor before purchasing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Avrenor Stays cost?

Avrenor Stays uses portfolio-based monthly pricing: $49/month for 1–3 properties, $129 for 4–10, $229 for 11–20, $399 for 21–40, $599 for 41–75, $799 for 76–100, and custom volume pricing for 100+ properties.

Does Avrenor Stays charge per property?

No. Avrenor Stays uses portfolio tier pricing rather than per-property billing. The effective cost per property declines as you move into larger tiers, making it more economical as a portfolio grows.

What is included in the Avrenor Stays subscription?

The subscription includes the core operating workspace: reservations, calendar, turnovers, properties, guests, expenses and performance. Confirm current feature inclusions, onboarding costs, payment processing and any optional modules directly with Avrenor before purchasing.

Is there an Avrenor Stays free trial?

Avrenor Stays offers demos. Visit avrenorstays.com to check current trial or onboarding options directly with the vendor, as availability can change.
