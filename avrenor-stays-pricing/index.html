Avrenor pricing explained

The pricing model is structured around portfolio size rather than charging the same per-property rate at every level. As the portfolio grows, the effective monthly software cost per property declines.

Which Avrenor plan fits your portfolio?

1–10 properties: suited to smaller hosts and operators consolidating core workflows. 11–40: aimed at professional managers coordinating a growing operating team. 41–100: creates lower effective per-property economics for larger portfolios. Operators above 100 properties move to volume pricing.

What to confirm before buying

Software price is only one part of a PMS decision. Confirm onboarding, integrations, payment processing, direct-booking requirements, support and any optional modules directly with the vendor before purchasing.
