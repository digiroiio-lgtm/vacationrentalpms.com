Frequently asked questions

What is vacation rental reporting software?

Vacation rental reporting software transforms reservation and operational data into daily summaries and period performance reports covering revenue, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, channel contribution and property-level results. It turns raw PMS data into management information.

What reports should a vacation rental PMS produce?

Core reports include: daily overview with arrivals, departures and occupancy; monthly revenue and performance summary; property-level ADR, RevPAR and booked nights; channel contribution by bookings and revenue; operating cost and net profit by property; and exportable reports for finance.

How is vacation rental reporting different from analytics?

Reporting covers what happened — daily operational summaries, period revenue totals, arrivals and departures. Analytics adds trend analysis, period-over-period comparisons, property-level benchmarking and market context. Many platforms combine both.

What export formats should vacation rental reporting support?

At minimum, look for PDF and Excel or CSV export options so reports can be shared with owners, accountants and finance teams. Confirm which specific reports are exportable, as support varies by platform.
