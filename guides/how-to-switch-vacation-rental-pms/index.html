MIGRATION GUIDE

How to Switch Vacation Rental PMS

A safe PMS migration protects future reservations first, then rebuilds channel, property, team and reporting workflows in a controlled sequence.

1. Inventory the current system

List properties, future bookings, guest data, channel connections, integrations, automation, cleaner workflows, reports and permissions.

2. Define acceptance criteria

Turn the reasons for switching into testable requirements for the destination PMS.

3. Plan data migration

Decide what history must move, what can remain archived and how future reservations will be validated.

4. Reconnect channels carefully

Avoid overlapping sources of truth. Confirm availability and reservation integrity during the cutover.

5. Train and test the operating day

Test arrivals, departures, turnovers, changes, cancellations and reporting before declaring the migration complete.
