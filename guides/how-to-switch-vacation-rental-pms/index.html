MIGRATION GUIDE

How to Switch Vacation Rental PMS

Short answer: Switch vacation rental PMS software by inventorying your current reservations and channel connections, defining what the new system must do, migrating data, reconnecting channels carefully to avoid overlap, and validating all workflows before go-live. Protect future bookings first; historical data can stay archived.

A safe PMS migration protects future reservations first, then rebuilds channel, property, team and reporting workflows in a controlled sequence.

1. Inventory the current system

List properties, future bookings, guest data, channel connections, integrations, automation, cleaner workflows, reports and permissions.

2. Define acceptance criteria

Turn the reasons for switching into testable requirements for the destination PMS.

3. Plan data migration

Decide what history must move, what can remain archived and how future reservations will be validated.

4. Reconnect channels carefully

Avoid overlapping sources of truth. Confirm availability and reservation integrity during the cutover.

5. Train and test the operating day

Test arrivals, departures, turnovers, changes, cancellations and reporting before declaring the migration complete.

Frequently asked questions

How do you switch vacation rental PMS software?

A safe PMS switch requires five steps: inventory your current system (properties, reservations, channel connections), define testable acceptance criteria, plan data migration, reconnect booking channels carefully to avoid double-bookings, then validate all workflows before going live.

How long does a vacation rental PMS migration take?

PMS migrations typically take 2–8 weeks depending on portfolio size, channel complexity and how much historical data needs migrating. A simple migration with clean data can complete in a few days; enterprise migrations may take months.

What is the biggest risk when switching vacation rental PMS?

The biggest risk is channel overlap during cutover — both the old and new PMS controlling the same calendar simultaneously, which can cause double-bookings. Validate that only one system controls availability on each channel before completing the switch.

What data needs to migrate when switching vacation rental PMS?

At minimum, migrate future reservations, guest contact records, property details and channel connection credentials. Historical bookings, owner statements and financial records may remain in the old system for reference rather than migrating fully.
