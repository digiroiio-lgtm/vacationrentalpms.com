TECH STACK GUIDE

Short-Term Rental Software: Build the Right Technology Stack

The goal of a short-term rental tech stack is not to use more software. It is to connect the fewest systems needed to distribute inventory, operate stays, automate work and understand performance.

1. Property management system

The PMS is typically the operational core. It stores reservation and property data and coordinates workflows that other tools consume.

2. Channel management

Distribution software synchronizes rates and availability across major booking channels. This may be built into the PMS or connected separately.

3. Revenue and dynamic pricing

Pricing tools use demand, seasonality and market signals to recommend or automate nightly rates and restrictions.

4. Guest experience and operations

Messaging, digital guidebooks, smart access, cleaning coordination and maintenance tools can extend the PMS where specialized workflows matter.

5. Direct booking and payments

A direct booking website can reduce dependence on marketplaces, while payment systems handle deposits, balances and transaction workflows.

6. Reporting, accounting and automation

As portfolios grow, clean financial exports, owner reporting, APIs and automation become more important. Prefer integrations that reduce duplicate data entry and preserve one reliable source of truth.
