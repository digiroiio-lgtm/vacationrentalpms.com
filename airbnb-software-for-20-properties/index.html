Software Stack for 20 Airbnb Properties

At 20 properties, the stack should support a professional operation. The question is no longer whether software saves clicks. It is whether the team can see and control the operating day.

Frequently asked questions

What software stack do I need for 20 Airbnb properties?

A 20-property professional stack typically includes: a PMS as the central operating system, multi-channel distribution, structured housekeeping and turnover management, team permissions and accountability, a pricing or revenue-management tool, expense tracking, and financial reporting with accounting integration.

What PMS fits a 20-property Airbnb portfolio?

At 20 properties, choose a PMS built for professional operations, not just host tools. Key criteria: operations-first design connecting reservations, turnovers and performance; owner reporting if managing third-party properties; scalable pricing; and a growth path toward 50+ properties without forced migration.

Do I need separate tools for each function at 20 properties?

Not necessarily. Many PMS platforms combine channel management, housekeeping, team workflows and reporting. The question is whether the PMS depth in each module matches your operational needs, or whether specialist tools add more value than the integration cost they introduce.

How much does software cost for 20 vacation rental properties?

PMS software for a 20-property portfolio typically costs $200–$600/month depending on the platform, pricing model and features. Always compare total cost including per-property fees, booking engine charges, payment processing and add-ons.
