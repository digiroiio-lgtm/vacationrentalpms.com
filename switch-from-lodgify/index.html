Frequently asked questions

How do you switch from Lodgify?

Switching from Lodgify requires inventorying property data, documenting direct-booking and channel dependencies, deciding on the direct-booking strategy in the new system, then running a controlled cutover that validates availability and reservation integrity.

What is unique about migrating from Lodgify?

Lodgify integrates website, direct-booking and PMS functions, so migrations require a clear decision about whether the new PMS will own the direct-booking layer or whether a separate website tool will remain in the stack.
