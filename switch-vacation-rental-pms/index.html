PMS MIGRATION

How to Switch Vacation Rental PMS

Changing PMS is a high-intent buying decision, but migration risk can keep teams on software they have outgrown. Map the operational data and workflows before choosing the replacement.

Compare PMS alternatives →

Signs it may be time to switch

Your team relies on spreadsheets beside the PMS

Turnovers and cleaner work live in separate tools

Daily operational problems are difficult to see

Reporting requires manual reconciliation

The platform is too complex or too limited for your portfolio

Vacation rental PMS migration checklist

Inventory reservations, guest records, properties, channel connections, calendar data, team permissions, cleaning workflows, expenses, reporting requirements and any integrations that are essential to the business.

Run the systems in parallel where practical

Before the final cutover, verify future reservations, availability, property data and operational responsibilities. Define who owns each migration step and how exceptions will be handled.

Evaluate the replacement around the operating day

Do not compare only feature checklists. Test how quickly managers can understand today's arrivals, departures, turnovers, assignments and performance. The new PMS should remove operational work, not simply move it.
