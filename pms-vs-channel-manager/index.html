Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a PMS and a channel manager?

A channel manager focuses on distribution: synchronizing rates, availability and reservations across booking platforms like Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com. A vacation rental PMS does everything a channel manager does plus manages the full operational workflow — reservations, properties, guests, housekeeping, teams, reporting and owner management.

Do I need both a PMS and a channel manager?

Not necessarily. Most modern vacation rental PMS platforms include channel management with native API connections to major OTAs. A separate channel manager may be needed if you require connections to many niche channels not covered by your PMS.

Can a channel manager replace a PMS?

No. A channel manager handles distribution and availability sync, but does not manage reservations, guest records, housekeeping workflows, team tasks, owner reporting or accounting. Growing operators typically need a PMS to run their operational day.

Which is better: PMS or channel manager?

For operators managing multiple properties or teams, a PMS is almost always more valuable because it manages the full operational workflow — not just channel distribution. A channel manager alone leaves reservations, guests, housekeeping and reporting in separate disconnected tools.
