Direct Booking vs Daily Operations

Getting the booking and operating the stay are different problems. Lodgify may be preferable when building a direct-booking presence is central to the buying decision. Avrenor is designed for teams whose central challenge is coordinating what happens across the portfolio every day.

Reservation Management

Both product categories need a reliable reservation layer. Avrenor connects reservations directly to calendar, guests, properties, turnovers and performance.

Turnover Operations & Team Coordination

Avrenor gives turnover coordination a prominent role, including cleaner assignment, job status, cleaner pay and team visibility.

Who Should Choose Lodgify?

Lodgify may be preferable for operators who place high value on vacation rental website creation and direct-booking capabilities alongside PMS functionality.

Who Should Choose Avrenor?

Avrenor is designed for professional operators, especially teams around 10–100 properties, that want the operating day and portfolio execution to be the center of the system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Avrenor is specifically designed around reservations, properties, turnovers, teams, expenses and performance in one operational workspace.

Yes. Its primary target is professional short-term rental teams managing approximately 10–100 properties.
