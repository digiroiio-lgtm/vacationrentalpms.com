Platform Philosophy

Hostaway provides a broad vacation rental technology platform. Avrenor deliberately focuses the interface around daily property operations. The choice is less about which has “more” and more about which operating model fits the team.

Reservation Management

Hostaway is built around professional vacation rental management and distribution. Avrenor connects its reservation workspace to calendar, properties, guests, turnovers, expenses and performance.

Daily Property Operations

Avrenor's product architecture makes today's arrivals, departures, turnover work and portfolio activity immediately visible, then lets teams move into the relevant operational workspace.

Turnovers and Team Coordination

Cleaner assignment, job status, cleaner pay and team access are central Avrenor workflows for teams that need tight execution after a booking is confirmed.

Who Should Choose Hostaway?

Hostaway may be preferable for property managers prioritizing a broad PMS and channel-management ecosystem as they scale.

Who Should Choose Avrenor?

Avrenor may be preferable for professional teams, especially around 10–100 properties, that want a simpler operational workspace and do not need maximum platform breadth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between Hostaway and Avrenor?

Hostaway offers a broad PMS and channel-management platform. Avrenor emphasizes a focused operations-first workspace.

Is Avrenor a Hostaway alternative?

Yes. It is particularly relevant for operations-focused professional property managers comparing PMS platforms.

Which platform is better for 10–100 properties?

The answer depends on required platform breadth. Avrenor specifically targets professional teams in approximately this portfolio range.

What should I compare before switching?

Compare channels, reservations, calendar, turnovers, team workflows, reporting, integrations, migration effort, ease of operation and total cost.
