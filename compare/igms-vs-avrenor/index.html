Where iGMS Is Strong

iGMS has long focused on vacation rental management and automation. Operators evaluating it are typically looking for established workflows around reservations, guest communication, channel-connected operations and team tasks.

Where Avrenor Is Different

Avrenor organizes the product around the operating day. Reservations, properties, turnovers, teams, expenses and performance are intended to remain visible as connected parts of one operational workflow.

Property Operations

Avrenor's differentiation is not simply another feature list. Its core proposition is an operating layer where managers can see what needs attention across the portfolio today.

Team & Turnover Management

Turnovers, cleaner assignments, cleaner pay, job status and team access are treated as core property operations rather than isolated tasks.

Which Platform Fits Your Portfolio?

iGMS may suit teams that prefer an established vacation rental automation platform. Avrenor may be preferable for professional operators, particularly around 10–100 properties, who want a leaner operating workflow centered on daily portfolio control.
