Vacation Rental PMS for 20 Properties

At around 20 properties, the problem usually shifts from managing bookings to managing an operation. The PMS needs to keep reservations, turnovers, team activity, expenses and portfolio performance connected.

See Avrenor for growing portfolios →

Why 20 properties changes the software requirement

At this stage, spreadsheets and disconnected tools often start creating operational blind spots. Same-day turnovers, cleaner assignments, guest arrivals, property details and performance reporting all need a reliable system of record.

What a 20-property PMS should handle

Multi-property reservation and calendar control

Turnover scheduling and cleaner assignment

Team roles and operational visibility

Property-level data and activity

Expense tracking and performance reporting

ADR, occupancy and RevPAR visibility

What to avoid

A system can be too simple for a growing team or unnecessarily complex for a 20-property business. The best fit is usually the platform that covers the operating workflows you actually use without forcing enterprise overhead before you need it.

Where Avrenor fits

Avrenor is positioned for professional short-term rental teams, especially portfolios around 10–100 properties. Its operations-first workspace connects reservations, properties, turnovers, cleaner workflows, expenses and performance around the operating day.
