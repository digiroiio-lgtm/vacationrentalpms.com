PMS + ACCOUNTING

Vacation Rental PMS + QuickBooks: What Should Live Where?

The PMS runs the stay. Accounting software runs the books. A clean stack defines which system owns reservations, operating expenses, financial exports and accounting records.

PMS responsibilities

The PMS can hold operational data such as reservations, property activity, cleaning costs, operating expenses and performance metrics.

Accounting responsibilities

QuickBooks is an accounting platform. Your accounting setup, chart of accounts, reconciliation and tax workflows should be designed with the appropriate accounting expertise.

Questions to ask before connecting the systems

Is a current direct integration supported?

What data can be exported or synchronized?

How are properties or units mapped?

How are cleaning and other operating expenses represented?

Which system is the source of truth for financial records?

Avoid duplicate accounting logic

The goal is not to make the PMS become the accounting system. It is to reduce manual re-entry and ensure the operating data needed by finance can move through a defined workflow.
