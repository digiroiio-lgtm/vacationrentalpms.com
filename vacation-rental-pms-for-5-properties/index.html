Frequently asked questions

What PMS should I use for 5 vacation rentals?

For 5 vacation rentals, choose a PMS that covers unified reservations and calendar, turnover and cleaner workflows, basic automation and portfolio reporting. Prioritize removing repeated manual work over enterprise complexity. Ensure the pricing model scales to 10–20 properties without forcing a platform switch.

Is a PMS necessary at 5 vacation rental properties?

A PMS is not mandatory at 5 properties, but five is a common transition point. The strongest reason to adopt one is repeated manual work: when a booking change requires updating multiple calendars, notifying cleaners and reconciling spreadsheets, a PMS typically pays for itself.

What PMS features matter most at 5 vacation rental properties?

Core features at this stage: unified reservation calendar, turnover creation from bookings, cleaner assignment and status, automated guest messages, basic reporting and a path to scale. Avoid paying for owner management, advanced permissions or enterprise accounting before you need them.

How much does a vacation rental PMS cost for 5 properties?

A vacation rental PMS for 5 properties typically costs $50–$150/month depending on the platform and pricing model. Compare total cost including per-property fees, payment processing charges, booking engine fees and any required add-ons.
